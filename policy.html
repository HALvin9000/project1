Policy

Returns & Exchanges

  • Component Policy: 15-day return window for unopened CPUs, GPUs, and motherboards.
  • Restocking Fee: 15% fee applied to opened but non-defective items.
  • Final Sale: No returns on custom-cut liquid cooling tubes or thermal paste.
  • Software: No returns on operating systems or game keys once scratched.

Warranty & Defective Items

  • Dead-on-Arrival: Exchange within 30 days for items that fail to power on.
  • Manufacturer Warranty: Customers must contact manufacturers directly after 30 days.
  • Physical Damage: Bent CPU pins or cracked PCB boards void all store warranties.
  • Overclocking: Damage caused by improper voltage modifications is not covered.

Custom PC Assembly

  • Build Time: Standard custom PC assembly takes 3 to 5 business days.
  • Testing: Every custom system undergoes a mandatory 12-hour stress test.
  • Parts Storage: Leftover component boxes will be kept for up to 14 days.
  • Service Fee: Non-refundable diagnostic fee of $49 applied to all repair evaluations.

Purchasing Limits

  • Graphics Cards: Maximum of 1 high-end GPU per customer per household.
  • Bulk Orders: Orders over 5 identical items require manager approval.
  • Price Matching: We match local brick-and-mortar competitors on identical in-stock items.
